## 🕹️ About

Dear Passengers is a first-person online co-op action-adventure game from FLEXUS, where you and your friends crew the world's worst airline. Pilot the aircraft, manage the cabin, handle risky cargo, and survive physics-driven chaos caused by turbulence, weather, and unruly passengers. The game is planned for release in 2026 and this beta test gives selected players the chance to help shape the flight before launch with a free game key.

Dear Passengers is made for friends who enjoy chaotic online co-op, physics-based comedy, teamwork, and high-pressure problem solving.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- ✈️ **Co-op Airline Crew** — Work together online as pilots and cabin crew.
- 🎮 **Pilot or Serve** — Fly the plane or keep the cabin under control.
- 📦 **Risky Cargo** — Choose high-paying cargo that can make every flight more dangerous.
- 🌪️ **Dynamic Flight Chaos** — Face turbulence, air pockets, weather, and physics-based disasters.
- 🧳 **Passenger Management** — Handle demanding travelers and prevent problems from escalating.
- 💥 **Emergent Comedy** — Turn unexpected failures into unforgettable team moments.
